TFL Northern Line

The Northern Line is one of the busiest lines on the London Underground, running north to south through the city with several branches.

  • Key stations: King's Cross St Pancras, Euston, Bank, London Bridge
  • Branches: Edgware, High Barnet, Mill Hill East, Charing Cross and Bank
  • Frequency: Trains run every few minutes during peak hours
